Reader orientation

How to use Daily Support Guide

Daily Support Guide is for calm reading and comparison. It helps you copy label facts, notice missing details, and keep ordinary decisions from becoming noisy.

Start with the type of question

If you are reading a label, use the label-reading guides. If you are comparing two similar options, use the comparison guides. If you are organizing a small daily habit, use the routine guides. If you are looking at nutrient lines, use the nutrient notes as general label education.

Keep notes factual

A useful note usually includes the source, date checked, serving size, amount, unit, percent Daily Value when shown, claim wording, and what the label does not answer.

Use nutrient notes carefully

Nutrient articles can help you read terms such as iron, vitamin D, calcium, magnesium, potassium, vitamin B12, folate, zinc, omega-3, and fiber on labels. They do not tell you whether you are low in a nutrient, whether you need a supplement, or what dose is right for you.

Stop when the question becomes personal

Questions about symptoms, medical conditions, pregnancy, medication use, lab results, allergies, diet plans, or treatment choices belong with a qualified professional. The site can help you organize what a label says before that conversation.

Use the site in this order

  1. Open the topic index and choose the closest guide area.
  2. Read one guide, not every guide at once.
  3. Copy only neutral facts from the label or page you are checking.
  4. Write down what is still unclear.
  5. Bring personal health questions to a qualified professional.

What this site does not do

Daily Support Guide does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure conditions. It does not recommend brands, rank products, provide affiliate links, or tell readers what to buy.
