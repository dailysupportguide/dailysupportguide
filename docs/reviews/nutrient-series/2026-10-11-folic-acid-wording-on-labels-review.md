## Source Notes

- FDA Daily Value guidance: the Daily Value for folate/folic acid is 400 mcg DFE.
- FDA folate and folic acid guidance: DFE means dietary folate equivalents.
- FDA Nutrition Facts guidance: percent Daily Value shows how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a daily diet.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements folate information: the Daily Value for folate is 400 mcg DFE for adults and children age 4 years and older.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements folate information: labels must list folate content in mcg DFE per serving, and if folic acid is added, the label must also list folic acid in mcg in parentheses.
- 21 CFR 101.54: high, rich in, or excellent source claims generally indicate 20 percent or more of the Daily Value per reference amount; good source, contains, or provides claims generally indicate 10 to 19 percent.
