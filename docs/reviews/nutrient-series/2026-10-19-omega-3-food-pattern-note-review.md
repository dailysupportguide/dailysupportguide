## Source Notes

- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements omega-3 information: omega-3 fatty acids include ALA, EPA, and DHA.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements omega-3 information: current Adequate Intakes for omega-3s are listed in grams per day; adult AIs are 1.6 g for males and 1.1 g for females as ALA.
- FDA Daily Value guidance: percent Daily Value shows how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a daily diet; required Nutrition Facts listings do not include EPA, DHA, or ALA.
- FDA omega-3 nutrient content claim guidance: certain nutrient content claims for DHA and EPA are prohibited; some ALA claim wording has been allowed to remain on the market when tied to a 1.6 g Daily Value for ALA.
- FDA EPA/DHA qualified health claim update: qualified claims require qualifying language because FDA concluded the evidence for listed blood-pressure and coronary-heart-disease wording is inconsistent and inconclusive.
