## Source Notes

- FDA Daily Value guidance: the Daily Value for vitamin D is 20 mcg for adults and children 4 years and older.
- FDA Nutrition Facts guidance: percent Daily Value shows how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a daily diet.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements vitamin D information: FDA requires food labels to list vitamin D in mcg per serving and allows IU in parentheses.
- FDA label guidance: vitamin D, calcium, iron, and potassium must declare actual amounts and percent Daily Value on the label.
- FDA structure/function claim guidance: structure/function wording may describe the role of a nutrient in normal body structure or function, but it is different from a disease claim.
