## Source Notes

- FDA Daily Value guidance: the Daily Value for magnesium is 420 mg for adults and children 4 years and older.
- FDA Nutrition Facts guidance: percent Daily Value shows how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a daily diet.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements magnesium information: FDA does not require food labels to list magnesium content unless magnesium has been added to the food.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements consumer information: magnesium is found naturally in many foods and is added to some fortified foods.
- 21 CFR 101.54: high, rich in, or excellent source claims generally indicate 20 percent or more of the Daily Value per reference amount; good source, contains, or provides claims generally indicate 10 to 19 percent.
