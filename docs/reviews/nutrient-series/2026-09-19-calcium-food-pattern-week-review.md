## Source Notes

- FDA Daily Value guidance: the Daily Value for calcium is 1,300 mg for adults and children 4 years and older.
- FDA Nutrition Facts guidance: percent Daily Value shows how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a daily diet.
- FDA label guidance: vitamin D, calcium, iron, and potassium must declare actual amounts and percent Daily Value on the label.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements calcium information: FDA requires food labels to list calcium content, and the Daily Value for calcium is 1,300 mg for adults and children age 4 years and older.
- 21 CFR 101.54: high, rich in, or excellent source claims generally indicate 20 percent or more of the Daily Value per reference amount; good source, contains, or provides claims generally indicate 10 to 19 percent.
