## Safety Frame

- Do not write "if you have X, you are deficient in Y."
- Do not make a symptom checker.
- Do not recommend supplements, products, brands, forms, or dosages.
- Use food records, label facts, intake patterns, and open questions.
- Include licensed professional guidance for personal or medical questions.
