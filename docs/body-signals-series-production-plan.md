# Everyday Body Signals Production Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines the next 50-article content line after the nutrient series.

Working title: Everyday Body Signals

Editorial promise: help readers notice everyday food-pattern and routine clues without diagnosing symptoms, recommending products, giving supplement advice, or implying that an article can determine a personal health issue.

## Scope

- 50 English articles.
- Five existing site authors, 10 articles each.
- Draft in 5-article batches.
- Each batch must pass internal polish before external review.
- External review quorum remains ChatGPT + Gemini + Claude.
- Grok is not part of the required gate.
- Do not publish directly from drafting or review tasks.

## Series Safety Rules

This series is closer to symptoms and body feelings than the nutrient label series, so the safety boundary is stricter.

- Do not say a body signal means the reader lacks a nutrient.
- Do not create symptom checkers.
- Do not list symptoms as proof of deficiency.
- Do not recommend supplements, doses, forms, brands, products, stores, tests, or treatments.
- Do not advise medication changes, supplement changes, or medical decisions.
- Use food-pattern, meal rhythm, routine, hydration, sleep, stress, and check-in framing.
- Use cautious language: "may be worth noticing", "can be part of the picture", "one place to start", "worth discussing with a licensed professional".
- Separate personal health factors: pregnancy, breastfeeding, medications, allergies, eating disorder history, chronic conditions, surgery, and individualized nutrition plans.
- End each article with the required wording: "This article is general education only and is not medical advice."

## Anti-AI Voice Rules

- Avoid template openings across articles.
- Avoid generic wellness language such as "optimize", "unlock", "journey", "empower", "holistic", and "transform".
- Avoid identical section rhythm across a batch.
- Avoid over-polished reassurance.
- Prefer concrete food records, ordinary daily scenes, and small human transitions.
- Keep author voice visible without becoming gimmicky.

## Production Workflow

1. Select one 5-article batch.
2. Draft each article in the assigned author voice.
3. Run internal safety and anti-AI polish.
4. Create review packet for each article.
5. Send quick external review to ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude only.
6. If all three pass, mark as approval candidate.
7. Stop and report before approval, scheduling, commit, push, or publication unless the user explicitly authorizes that step.
