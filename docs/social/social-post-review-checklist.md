# Social Post Review Checklist

Status: active checklist  
Project: Daily Support Guide  
Effective date: 2026-08-18

Use this checklist before any Daily Support Guide social draft is approved for posting.

## Required Metadata

- Platform:
- Source article or page:
- Draft owner:
- Review date:
- Reviewer:
- Status: `PASS`, `PASS WITH EDITS`, or `BLOCK`

## Editorial Safety

The draft must pass every item:

- No medical advice.
- No diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, or disease claim.
- No symptom-to-nutrient implication.
- No supplement, dosage, product, brand, retailer, or affiliate recommendation.
- No statement that implies a nutrient note can decide personal health needs.
- No request for readers to share symptoms, lab results, diagnoses, pregnancy status, medication use, allergies, or personal nutrition plans.
- No fear hook or urgent health framing.
- No before/after claim.
- No hidden sponsored, affiliate, or endorsement language.

## Platform Fit

Threads:

- One idea only.
- Sounds like a person, not a campaign.
- Useful even without a click.
- Does not ask for personal health details.

Instagram:

- 5 to 8 slides when drafted as carousel.
- One thought per slide.
- Good for saving.
- No tiny text or overloaded slide.

Facebook:

- Clear summary.
- Calm and credible.
- Suitable for a Page post.
- Does not invite medical Q&A in comments.

## Voice Check

The draft should:

- Be plain and specific.
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid generic AI phrasing.
- Keep the reader's agency.
- Match Daily Support Guide's neutral education tone.

Prefer:

- "Copy the serving size before comparing amounts."
- "Keep claim wording separate from the number."
- "Stop when the question becomes personal."

Avoid:

- "Transform your wellness journey."
- "This nutrient could be the missing piece."
- "Everyone needs to know this."
- "DM us your symptoms."

## CTA Check

Allowed CTAs:

- "Read the full guide."
- "Browse the topic index."
- "Use this as a label-reading note."
- "Bring personal questions to a qualified professional."

Not allowed:

- "Buy this."
- "Shop our picks."
- "Use our code."
- "Tell us your symptoms."
- "Try this dose."

## Final Decision

`PASS` means the draft can be sent to the founder for posting approval.

`PASS WITH EDITS` means the draft needs small edits before founder review.

`BLOCK` means the draft should not be posted without rewriting and re-review.
