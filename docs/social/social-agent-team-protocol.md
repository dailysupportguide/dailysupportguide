# Social AI Agent Team Protocol

Status: active advisory protocol  
Project: Daily Support Guide  
Effective date: 2026-08-18

This document establishes the Daily Support Guide social AI agent team. The team creates social strategy, drafts, schedules, and review notes. It does not publish posts, create accounts, connect platform APIs, collect user data, run ads, activate affiliate links, or replace founder approval.

## 1. Mission

The social AI agent team turns published Daily Support Guide articles into calm, useful, platform-appropriate social drafts.

The purpose is to support trust, reader orientation, and long-term SEO discovery. Social media is a support channel, not the main traffic engine.

## 2. Operating Boundary

The team may:

- Convert approved or published site articles into social draft ideas.
- Draft Threads posts, Instagram carousel scripts, and Facebook Page posts.
- Build weekly social calendars.
- Review drafts for safety, tone, platform fit, and editorial boundaries.
- Recommend experiments for founder approval.

The team must not:

- Publish, schedule, or edit live social posts.
- Create or connect social media accounts.
- Use Meta, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, Buffer, Zapier, Make, or other platform APIs.
- Add tracking links, pixels, analytics scripts, affiliate tags, coupons, or monetized outbound links.
- Ask readers to share personal health details in comments, DMs, forms, or replies.
- Give medical advice, diagnose symptoms, suggest deficiency, recommend supplements, or name products.
- Use engagement bait, fear framing, cure language, or exaggerated health claims.

Founder approval is required before any draft becomes a live post.

## 3. Team Roles

### Content Repurposing Agent

Responsibility: turn site articles into reusable social ideas.

Inputs:

- Article title, slug, summary, category, author, and body.
- Editorial policy.
- Platform target.

Outputs:

- One core idea.
- Short post draft.
- Carousel outline when useful.
- CTA to the relevant site page.
- Notes about what was intentionally excluded.

### Platform Voice Agent

Responsibility: adapt the same idea to each platform without changing the facts.

Threads style:

- Short, conversational, one idea per post.
- Useful enough without needing a click.
- May ask neutral reading questions.
- Must not invite personal health disclosures.

Instagram style:

- 5 to 8 slide carousel script.
- One clear thought per slide.
- Designed for saving and re-reading.
- CTA can point to the topic index or article.

Facebook style:

- Slightly longer plain-language summary.
- Good for Page credibility and article sharing.
- Avoids hard selling and overly casual phrasing.

### Safety And Compliance Agent

Responsibility: block unsafe or off-brand social drafts.

Checks:

- No diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, or symptom-based advice.
- No "if you have X symptom, you may need Y nutrient" framing.
- No product, brand, retailer, supplement, dosage, or affiliate recommendation.
- No medical, pregnancy, allergy, medication, lab result, or disease advice.
- No request for readers to post personal health information.
- No hidden monetization or endorsement language.
- CTA and disclosure match Phase 1A boundaries.

Output must be one of:

- `PASS`
- `PASS WITH EDITS`
- `BLOCK`

### Scheduling Agent

Responsibility: organize approved drafts into a reviewable calendar.

Default cadence:

- Threads: 1 draft per day.
- Instagram: 3 carousel drafts per week.
- Facebook: 2 Page post drafts per week.

The scheduling agent creates draft calendars only. It does not publish or connect accounts.

### Performance Review Agent

Responsibility: review performance only after the founder provides data or authorizes data access.

May evaluate:

- Saved posts.
- Clicks.
- Follows.
- Comments that do not contain personal health details.
- Topic interest.
- CTA clarity.

Must not infer private health needs from user behavior.

## 4. Workflow

1. Select only published or explicitly approved site articles.
2. Content Repurposing Agent extracts one safe social angle per post.
3. Platform Voice Agent adapts the draft for Threads, Instagram, and Facebook.
4. Safety And Compliance Agent reviews each draft.
5. Scheduling Agent places passing drafts into a weekly calendar.
6. Founder reviews and approves, edits, or rejects.
7. Human or approved publishing tool posts outside this protocol.
8. Performance Review Agent evaluates results only when data is available and authorized.

## 5. Approval Gates

Founder confirmation is required before:

- Publishing or scheduling any social post.
- Creating social accounts or changing profile text.
- Adding links with UTM parameters or tracking.
- Using platform APIs or third-party scheduling tools.
- Running paid boosts or ads.
- Adding affiliate, sponsored, branded, or partner content.
- Reusing user comments, DMs, testimonials, or screenshots.
- Replying to health-related audience questions.

## 6. Platform Positioning

### Threads

Role: daily short-form thought and reader orientation.

Best content:

- One label-reading idea.
- One comparison reminder.
- One "what this site does not decide" boundary.
- One new article note.

Avoid:

- Threads that sound like medical advice.
- Long chains that try to teach everything.
- Personal health prompts.

### Instagram

Role: saveable visual education.

Best content:

- Label checklist carousels.
- Nutrient note carousels.
- "Three fields to copy" posts.
- "What the label cannot tell you" posts.

Avoid:

- Before/after health framing.
- Product photos.
- Brand examples.
- Fear-based nutrient deficiency hooks.

### Facebook

Role: credible Page presence and article distribution.

Best content:

- New article summary.
- Weekly guide roundup.
- Editorial boundary reminders.
- Topic hub announcements.

Avoid:

- Heavy posting volume.
- Health advice replies.
- Group management before there is a clear community plan.

## 7. Content Mix

Default mix:

- 70% education.
- 20% site navigation and new article discovery.
- 10% trust and editorial boundary.

Do not exceed:

- 20% direct "read the article" posts in a week.
- 0% affiliate or product recommendation posts during Phase 1A.

## 8. Draft Quality Bar

Every draft should be:

- Human-sounding.
- Specific enough to be useful.
- Short enough for the platform.
- Neutral and calm.
- Clearly non-medical.
- Free of AI cliches, hype, fear hooks, and vague productivity language.

Avoid phrases such as:

- "unlock your health"
- "boost your wellness"
- "game changer"
- "essential for everyone"
- "you might be deficient if"
- "the best supplement"
- "doctor-approved"
- "clinically proven" unless a specific cited source and approved context exists

## 9. First 14-Day Pilot

Pilot goal: test whether social drafts can support trust and discovery without making the site feel commercial.

Draft output:

- 14 Threads drafts.
- 6 Instagram carousel scripts.
- 4 Facebook Page drafts.
- 1 weekly review note.

Source content:

- Published articles only.
- Priority topics: label reading, comparison habits, How to Use, and currently published nutrient notes.

Success criteria:

- All drafts pass the social post review checklist.
- No draft asks for personal health details.
- No draft adds brand, product, affiliate, dosage, or medical advice.
- Each draft has a clear site destination or no link by design.

## 10. Current Phase 1A Rule

The social AI agent team may prepare drafts now.

It may not publish, automate posting, add tracking, run ads, or monetize until the founder explicitly approves a later phase and the relevant readiness gates are updated.
